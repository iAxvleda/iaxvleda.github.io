## A gate should help a team decide

A quality gate is useful when it is fast enough to be respected, stable enough to be trusted, and specific enough to explain what failed.

## Design principles

- Run deterministic, high-signal checks before expensive suites.
- Separate infrastructure failures from product failures.
- Quarantine flaky checks instead of teaching the team to ignore red builds.
- Publish the failed risk or user journey, not only a test name.
- Reserve blocking gates for evidence that should genuinely stop a release.

Good CI does not merely report test output. It turns evidence into a clear next action.
