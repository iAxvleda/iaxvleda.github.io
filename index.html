I work hands-on — manually and through automation — across mainstream browsers, testing frameworks, and programming languages.

More than a decade in software quality has taught me how to investigate unfamiliar products, find meaningful risk, and adapt the testing approach to the system rather than forcing the system into a favorite tool.

Five of those years were focused on automation inside a legacy Java and Maven project: the kind of environment where maintainability, patience, and learning the existing architecture matter as much as writing the next test.
